## HUNT 追擊距離 / HUNT pursuit range

**繁體中文：** GDD 對 HUNT 敵方槍兵規定追騎兵最多六格；原程式超距後可能在計時結束前繼續追擊，甚至透過一般索敵在七格內重新選回騎兵。現在當雙方距離超過六格立即重新選敵，且一般後備索敵不會選回超距騎兵；恰好六格仍可追擊。其他可用目標和核心仍依既有優先規則選擇；BREACH 優先級與非 HUNT 行為保留。這使用既有六格判斷，沒有新增平衡數值。觸發提早重選時會較早消耗既有重選 RNG，因此不宣稱與舊版隨機序列相同；同種子重播測試通過。

正式建置來源 `75af6250107b89fb9206726f3a87a05cc30405bb`，Chromium 驗證 127 個資源雜湊。固定場景以被動騎兵及原生右鍵移動比較：舊版距離 6.22 格仍追擊，新版距離 6.24 格轉向核心；截圖及 JSON 在 `content-e8b-evidence/hunt-*`。五個定向 Node 案例及六個相關回歸檔通過。首次瀏覽器嘗試因騎兵預設交戰站姿而未達測試距離，屬測試場景失敗；調整為被動站姿後重新驗證，沒有修改碰撞或完整性檢查。這不是自然戰役驗收；GDD 的視野限制及兩格分散隊形仍待實作與驗證，不能宣稱完整 HUNT 規則已完成。

**English:** The GDD specifies that HUNT enemy spearmen pursue cavalry for at most six tiles. Previously they could continue beyond that separation until their retarget timer expired or reacquire cavalry within seven tiles through the generic fallback. They now immediately retarget once separation exceeds six tiles, and the fallback cannot reacquire out-of-range cavalry; exactly six tiles remains eligible. Other available targets and the core retain existing priority rules; BREACH priority and non-HUNT behavior are preserved. This uses the existing six-tile comparison without adding balance values. Earlier retargeting can consume the existing retarget RNG earlier, so the old random sequence is not claimed to be identical; same-seed replay passed.

This is not natural-campaign acceptance. The GDD visibility restriction and two-tile spread formation still require implementation and validation; full HUNT compliance is not claimed.
