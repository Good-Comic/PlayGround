THE WAR COUNCIL

How to Rule

Gather

Select villagers and right-click trees, berries, gold, or stone. They haul every load back to the Town Center.

Build

With villagers selected, choose a structure from the command panel, then place it on open ground.

Fight

Select your soldiers and right-click a raider to attack. Idle troops defend themselves; towers rain arrows.

Endure

Survive ten raids. After each victory, claim one of three boons to shape your kingdom's fate.
